Reviewer names, freeform reasons and private receipt references are omitted. The [source manifest](source-manifest.json) records the derivative's hash and its original source hash.

Use these labels for exploratory comparison and descriptive error analysis. Numerical reproducibility does not establish independent human agreement, model superiority or whether a patch repaired an issue.
